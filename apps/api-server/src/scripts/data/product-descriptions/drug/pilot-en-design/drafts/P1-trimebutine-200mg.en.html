Over-the-counter Digestive

Trimebutine Maleate 200 mg TabletA digestive aid that regulates gut movement

Taken by mouth · Adults

This medicine is used for acid reflux and hiatal hernia; for the digestive problems that come with gastritis, duodenitis or ulcers — stomach pain, indigestion, nausea and vomiting; and for irritable bowel syndrome and spastic colon.

What it helps with

  • Acid reflux and hiatal hernia
  • Stomach pain, indigestion, nausea and vomiting with gastritis, duodenitis or ulcers
  • Irritable bowel syndrome and spastic colon

At a glance

Ingredient

Trimebutine maleate 200 mg

Category: over-the-counter medicine.

How it works

Regulates gut movement

Acts on the movement of the stomach and bowel.

Main symptoms

Stomach and bowel discomfort

Stomach pain, indigestion, nausea, vomiting, irritable bowel symptoms.

Why this one

A digestive aid

Chosen when gut movement needs regulating rather than acid reducing.

Who should be careful

Lactose metabolism disorders

Also pregnancy and breastfeeding — ask a pharmacist first.

Category

Over-the-counter

Available without a prescription at the pharmacy counter.

How to take it

Adults take 100–200 mg three times a day, before meals.The dose may be adjusted to suit your age and symptoms.

Before you take this

  • Do not take this if you have an inherited problem with lactose metabolism, such as galactose intolerance.
  • Talk to a pharmacist before taking it if you are pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • Talk to a pharmacist before taking it if you have ever reacted to Yellow No. 4 (tartrazine).
