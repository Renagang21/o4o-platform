Magnesium hydroxide 500 mg

This medicine is used to neutralise stomach acid and ease symptoms of stomach and duodenal ulcers, gastritis and excess stomach acid, and to relieve constipation.

How it works

Neutralises stomach acid and relieves constipation

Main symptoms

Heartburn, excess stomach acid, constipation

Who should be careful

Kidney problems, diarrhoea, high magnesium levels

Why this one

A magnesium preparation used both to neutralise acid and to relieve constipation

How to take it

To neutralise acid, take 2–5 tablets (1–2.5 g) a day in divided doses. For constipation, take 2–4 tablets (1–2 g) a day in one or two doses. The dose may be adjusted to suit your age and symptoms.

Before you take this

  • Do not take this if you have kidney problems or diarrhoea.
  • Talk to a pharmacist before taking it if you have heart problems or high magnesium levels in the blood, and do not take it together with tetracycline antibiotics.
  • Taking it with a lot of milk or calcium supplements can cause milk-alkali syndrome.
