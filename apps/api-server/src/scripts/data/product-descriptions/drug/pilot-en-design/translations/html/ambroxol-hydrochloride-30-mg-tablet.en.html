Ambroxol hydrochloride 30 mg

This medicine is used for sudden and long-lasting bronchitis and asthmatic bronchitis caused by problems with mucus production.

At a glance

Category

Over-the-counter

Ingredient

Ambroxol hydrochloride 30 mg

How it works

Helps you clear mucus

Main symptoms

Sudden and long-lasting bronchitis, asthmatic bronchitis from mucus problems

Who should be careful

Severe liver or kidney problems; early pregnancy

Why this one

A treatment that helps clear phlegm

How to take it

Adults take one tablet three times a day (twice a day once symptoms settle). It can be taken with or without food.

Before you take this

  • Do not take this if you have ever reacted to it, if you have severe liver or kidney problems, if you are in the first 3 months of pregnancy, or if you are breastfeeding.
  • Talk to a pharmacist before taking it if you are pregnant, elderly, or have a peptic ulcer.
  • Rarely a severe skin reaction can occur — stop taking it straight away if this happens.
