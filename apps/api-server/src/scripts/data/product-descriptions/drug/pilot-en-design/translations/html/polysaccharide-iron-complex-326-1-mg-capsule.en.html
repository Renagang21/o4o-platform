An iron treatment for anaemia

How to take it

Adults take one capsule (150 mg as iron) once a day. Avoid green tea, black tea, milk and dairy products just before and after taking it.

Before you take this

  • Do not take this if you have ever reacted to it, for a baby under 12 months, or if you have haemochromatosis, anaemia that is not caused by iron deficiency, or a problem using iron.
  • If a child takes too much, iron poisoning can be fatal — always keep it out of the reach of children.
  • Talk to a pharmacist before taking it if you have a peptic ulcer or kidney problems, and do not take it together with an antacid, tetracycline or a calcium supplement.
