L-carnitine 330 mg

This medicine is used for primary and secondary carnitine deficiency, and for problems with heart muscle metabolism caused by reduced blood supply to the heart, such as angina and sudden heart attack.

Over-the-counter

How it works

Supplies carnitine (improves heart muscle metabolism)

Main symptoms

Carnitine deficiency; heart muscle metabolism problems from reduced blood supply

Who should be careful

Pregnancy and breastfeeding

Why this one

A metabolic preparation that replaces carnitine

How to take it

Adults take 2–3 g a day, divided into two or three doses. The dose may be adjusted to suit your age and symptoms.

Before you take this

  • Do not take this if you have ever reacted to it.
  • Talk to a pharmacist before taking it if you are pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • If muscle weakness appears in a person with uraemia, stop taking it and get advice.
