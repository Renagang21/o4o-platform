Diosmin 300 mg

This medicine is used to ease heaviness and pain in the legs caused by poor vein circulation, as a supportive treatment for problems caused by fragile capillaries, and to treat the signs of haemorrhoids.

Improves vein circulation

Main symptoms

Who should be careful

Breastfeeding; long-term use

Why this one

A vein circulation treatment for heavy legs and haemorrhoid symptoms (capsule)

How to take it

Adults take one capsule twice a day. If haemorrhoids come back or get worse, up to 1,200–1,800 mg a day may be taken. The dose may be adjusted to suit your age and symptoms.

Before you take this

  • Do not take this if you have ever reacted to it, or if you are breastfeeding.
  • Talk to a pharmacist before taking it if you are pregnant.
  • Do not take it for a long period.
  • If your symptoms do not improve quickly, an examination of the anus and a review of your treatment are needed.
