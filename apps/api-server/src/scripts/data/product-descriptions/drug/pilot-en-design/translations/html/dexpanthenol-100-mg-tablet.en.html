Who should be careful

Under 19; not for any purpose other than hair loss

Why this one

A preparation used as a supportive treatment for hair loss

How to take it

Adults take one tablet three times a day. The treatment period is 6 weeks.

Before you take this

  • Do not take this if you have ever reacted to it, if you are under 19, or for any purpose other than hair loss.
  • Talk to a pharmacist before taking it if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, or have ever had a drug allergy.
  • If there is no improvement after about 6 weeks, stop taking it.
