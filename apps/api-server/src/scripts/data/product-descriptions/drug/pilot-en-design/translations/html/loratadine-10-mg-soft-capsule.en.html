Loratadine 10 mg

This medicine is used for allergic rhinitis (sneezing, a blocked nose, itching and burning eyes) and for long-lasting hives with no known cause.

At a glance

Category

Over-the-counter

Ingredient

Loratadine 10 mg

How it works

Antihistamine (eases allergy symptoms)

Main symptoms

Allergic rhinitis, long-lasting hives

Who should be careful

Under 6; pregnancy and breastfeeding; liver problems

Why this one

An antihistamine taken once a day (soft capsule)

How to take it

Adults and children 12 or older take one capsule (10 mg) once a day. Children aged 6 to under 12 who weigh 30 kg or more take 10 mg once a day.

Before you take this

  • Do not take this if you have ever reacted to it, if you are under 6, or if you are pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • Talk to a pharmacist before taking it if you have liver problems or kidney failure.
  • Do not take it together with cimetidine, erythromycin or ketoconazole.
