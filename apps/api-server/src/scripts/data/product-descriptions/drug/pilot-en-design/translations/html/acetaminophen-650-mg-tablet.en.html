Acetaminophen 650 mg (slow-release tablet)

This medicine is used to reduce fever and to ease pain from a cold, headache, toothache, muscle pain, back pain, period pain and joint pain.

At a glance

Category

Over-the-counter

Ingredient

Acetaminophen 650 mg (slow-release tablet)

How it works

Reduces fever and pain

Main symptoms

Fever, headache, toothache, muscle pain, back pain, period pain, joint pain

Who should be careful

Liver disease; alcohol; do not exceed 4,000 mg a day

Why this one

A slow-release fever and pain reliever for longer-lasting effect

How to take it

Adults and children 12 or older take two tablets every 8 hours. Do not take more than six tablets in 24 hours. This is a slow-release tablet, so swallow it whole — do not crush or chew it. Do not take it together with other cold or pain medicines that contain acetaminophen.

Before you take this

  • Do not take this if you have ever reacted to it, if you have a peptic ulcer, severe liver or kidney problems, aspirin-induced asthma, or if you are under 12.
  • If you drink alcohol regularly there is a risk of liver damage, so get advice, and do not take more than 4,000 mg a day.
  • Talk to a pharmacist or doctor if a fever lasts more than 3 days or pain more than 10 days.
