Erdosteine 300 mg

This medicine is used to thin mucus and help you cough it up in sudden and long-lasting breathing problems.

At a glance

Category

Over-the-counter

Ingredient

Erdosteine 300 mg

How it works

Thins mucus and helps you cough it up

Main symptoms

Thick phlegm in sudden and long-lasting breathing problems

Who should be careful

Peptic ulcer; severe kidney problems; cirrhosis of the liver

Why this one

A mucus-thinning treatment that helps clear phlegm (tablet)

How to take it

Adults take one tablet (300 mg) two to three times a day. For sudden breathing problems, do not take it for more than 10 days in a row.

Before you take this

  • Do not take this if you have ever reacted to it, if you have cirrhosis of the liver, cystathionine synthase deficiency, a peptic ulcer, or severe kidney problems.
  • Talk to a pharmacist before taking it if you have mild to moderate liver problems, or are pregnant or breastfeeding, and get advice before taking it with other cough or phlegm medicines.
