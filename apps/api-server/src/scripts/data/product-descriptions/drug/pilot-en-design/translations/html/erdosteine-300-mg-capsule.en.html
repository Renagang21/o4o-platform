Erdosteine 300 mg

This medicine is used in sudden and long-lasting breathing problems to thin phlegm so that it is easier to cough up.

At a glance

Category

Over-the-counter

Ingredient

Erdosteine 300 mg

How it works

Thins mucus and helps you cough it up

Main symptoms

Thick phlegm in sudden and long-lasting breathing problems; a cough that will not clear

Who should be careful

Peptic ulcer; severe kidney problems; cirrhosis of the liver; reaction to this medicine

Why this one

A mucus-thinning treatment that helps phlegm clear more easily

How to take it

Adults take one capsule (300 mg) two to three times a day. For sudden breathing problems, do not take it for more than 10 days in a row. Talk to a pharmacist before taking it with other cough or phlegm medicines.

Before you take this

  • Take care if you have ever reacted to this medicine, or if you have a peptic ulcer, severe kidney problems, cirrhosis of the liver, or cystathionine synthase deficiency.
  • Talk to a pharmacist before taking it if you have mild to moderate liver problems or have had an allergic reaction before.
  • Talk to a pharmacist or doctor if the phlegm or cough lasts a long time, or comes with a fever or difficulty breathing.
