Bromelain 45 mg

This medicine is used to ease inflammation that comes with swelling, and swelling after injury or surgery.

At a glance

Category

Over-the-counter

Ingredient

How it works

Anti-inflammatory enzyme (eases swelling)

Main symptoms

Inflammation with swelling; swelling after injury or surgery

Who should be careful

Blood clotting disorders; taking anticoagulants; pregnancy

Why this one

An enzyme treatment that eases inflammatory swelling (lower strength)

How to take it

Adults and children 12 or older take one to two tablets (45–90 mg) three times a day, with water, 30 minutes before meals.

Before you take this

  • Do not take this if you have a severe blood clotting disorder, severe liver or kidney disease, if you are taking anticoagulants or antiplatelet medicines, if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, or if you are under 12.
  • Talk to a pharmacist before taking it if you are taking antibiotics such as tetracycline, or have had an allergic reaction before.
