Nizatidine 75 mg TabletAn H2 blocker that reduces stomach acid

This medicine is used for excess stomach acid, heartburn and acid burping.

What it helps with

  • Excess stomach acid
  • Heartburn
  • Acid burping

At a glance

Ingredient

Nizatidine 75 mg

Category: over-the-counter medicine.

How it works

Reduces stomach acid

Blocks the H2 receptor so the stomach makes less acid.

Main symptoms

Excess acid, heartburn

Excess stomach acid, heartburn, acid burping.

Why this one

An H2 blocker

Chosen to reduce acid production rather than neutralise acid already there.

Who should be careful

Under 15 or 80 and over

Also liver or kidney disease.

Category

Over-the-counter

Available without a prescription at the pharmacy counter.

How to take it

Adults aged 15–79 take one tablet (75 mg) once a day. If your symptoms have not improved after 8 hours, you may take one more tablet.Do not take more than two tablets (150 mg) in one day. Do not drink alcohol while taking this medicine.

Before you take this

  • Do not take this if you are under 15, or 80 or older.
  • Do not take this if you have ever reacted to this medicine or to another H2 blocker.
  • Do not take this if you have a blood disorder, liver or kidney disease, or a stomach or duodenal condition.
  • Do not take this if you are pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • Talk to a pharmacist before taking it if you have a sore throat, cough or high fever; unexplained weight loss; or ongoing stomach pain.
