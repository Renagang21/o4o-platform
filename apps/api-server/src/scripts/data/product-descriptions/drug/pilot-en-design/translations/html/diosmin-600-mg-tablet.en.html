Diosmin 600 mg

This medicine is used for heaviness and pain in the legs caused by poor vein circulation, as a supportive treatment for problems caused by fragile capillaries, and to treat the signs of haemorrhoids.

At a glance

Category

Over-the-counter

Ingredient

Diosmin 600 mg

How it works

Improves vein circulation

Main symptoms

Heavy, aching legs from poor vein circulation; signs of haemorrhoids

Who should be careful

Breastfeeding; long-term use

Why this one

A vein circulation treatment for heavy legs and haemorrhoid symptoms

How to take it

For poor vein circulation, take one tablet (600 mg) once a day, before breakfast. For haemorrhoids, take one tablet two to three times a day with meals.

Before you take this

  • Do not take this if you have ever reacted to it, or if you are breastfeeding.
  • Talk to a pharmacist before taking it if you are pregnant.
  • Do not take it for a long period.
  • If your symptoms do not improve quickly, have an examination of the anus or talk to a pharmacist.
