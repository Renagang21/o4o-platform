Folic acid 1 mg

This medicine is used to prevent and treat folic acid deficiency, to supply folic acid during pregnancy and breastfeeding, and for anaemia linked to folic acid deficiency — such as anaemia from poor nutrition, anaemia in pregnancy, and anaemia in children.

At a glance

Category

Over-the-counter

Ingredient

Folic acid 1 mg

How it works

Supplies folic acid (folate)

Main symptoms

Folic acid deficiency; folic acid supply in pregnancy and breastfeeding; anaemia from poor nutrition

Who should be careful

Pernicious anaemia from vitamin B12 deficiency

Why this one

A vitamin preparation that replaces folate

How to take it

To treat folic acid deficiency, adults and children 24 months or older take 0.25–1 mg a day. To prevent it, or when the body needs more, take 0.1–0.25 mg a day.

Before you take this

  • Do not take this if you have ever reacted to it, or if you have pernicious anaemia or megaloblastic anaemia caused by vitamin B12 deficiency (it can mask the cause of the anaemia).
  • Talk to a pharmacist before taking it if you are under a doctor's care.
  • If there is no improvement after about a month, stop taking it.
