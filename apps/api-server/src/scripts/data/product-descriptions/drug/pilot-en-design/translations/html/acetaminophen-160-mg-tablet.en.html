Acetaminophen 160 mg

This medicine is used for fever and pain from a cold, headache, nerve pain, muscle pain, period pain, sprain pain, toothache and joint pain.

At a glance

Category

Over-the-counter

Ingredient

Acetaminophen 160 mg

How it works

Reduces fever and pain

Main symptoms

Fever, headache, muscle pain and toothache in children

Who should be careful

Dose is based on body weight; liver disease; other cold medicines taken at the same time

Why this one

A fever and pain reliever dosed by a child's body weight

How to take it

Children 12 or under take the recommended dose every 4 to 6 hours as needed. If the child's weight is known, 10–15 mg per kg of body weight is more suitable, and no more than five doses (75 mg/kg) a day. Check the exact dose for the child's age and weight with a pharmacist.

Before you take this

  • Do not take this if you have ever reacted to it, if you have severe liver or kidney problems, or aspirin-induced asthma.
  • Do not take it together with other products that contain acetaminophen.
  • Get advice if a fever lasts more than 3 days, or pain more than 5 days in a child.
  • A child should take it under the supervision of a guardian.
