Nifuroxazide 200 mg

This medicine is used for sudden diarrhoea caused by bacteria.

At a glance

Category

Over-the-counter

Ingredient

Nifuroxazide 200 mg

How it works

Acts on bacteria in the gut (anti-diarrhoeal)

Main symptoms

Sudden bacterial diarrhoea

Who should be careful

Under 18; do not take for more than 3 days

Why this one

An anti-diarrhoeal that works inside the gut

How to take it

Adults take one capsule (200 mg) four times a day. Do not take it for more than 3 days. Drink plenty of fluids while taking it.

Before you take this

  • Do not take this if you have ever reacted to it, or if you are a child or teenager under 18.
  • Talk to a pharmacist before taking it if you are pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • If the diarrhoea continues after 2 days of treatment, consider replacing lost fluids or get advice.
