Acetylcysteine 200 mg

This medicine is used when phlegm is hard to cough up in sudden or long-lasting bronchitis, asthma, inflammation of the voice box, or sinus inflammation.

At a glance

Category

Over-the-counter

Ingredient

Acetylcysteine 200 mg

How it works

Thins mucus and helps you cough it up

Main symptoms

Thick phlegm in bronchitis, inflammation of the voice box or sinuses

Who should be careful

Stomach or duodenal ulcer; under 2

Why this one

A mucus-thinning treatment that makes phlegm easier to clear

How to take it

For sudden illness, adults take one capsule (200 mg) three times a day. For long-lasting illness, twice a day, before meals, with a small amount of water. Leave at least 2 hours between this medicine and an antibiotic taken by mouth.

Before you take this

  • Do not take this if you have ever reacted to it, if you have a stomach or duodenal ulcer, or for a baby under 2.
  • Talk to a pharmacist before taking it if you are pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • Talk to a pharmacist or doctor if the phlegm or cough lasts a long time, or comes with a fever or difficulty breathing.
