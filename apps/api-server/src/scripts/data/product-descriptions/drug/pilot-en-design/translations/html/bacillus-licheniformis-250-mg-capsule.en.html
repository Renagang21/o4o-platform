Bacillus licheniformis 250 mg

This medicine is used for sudden and long-lasting bowel inflammation, sudden and long-lasting diarrhoea, sudden dysentery, and abnormal fermentation in the bowel from various causes.

At a glance

Category

Over-the-counter

Ingredient

Bacillus licheniformis 250 mg

How it works

Settles the gut (improves the balance of gut bacteria)

Main symptoms

Sudden and long-lasting bowel inflammation, diarrhoea, abnormal fermentation in the bowel

Who should be careful

Babies under 3 months

Why this one

A live-culture treatment that helps balance the gut

How to take it

Adults take two capsules three times a day (four capsules for the first dose). Children take one capsule three times a day. For infants, the contents of the capsule may be mixed with a small amount of water or milk.

Before you take this

  • Talk to a pharmacist before giving this to a baby under 3 months old.
  • Talk to a pharmacist if it is taken together with certain antibiotics such as ciprofloxacin.
  • Get advice if the diarrhoea lasts a long time or comes with a fever or blood in the stool.
