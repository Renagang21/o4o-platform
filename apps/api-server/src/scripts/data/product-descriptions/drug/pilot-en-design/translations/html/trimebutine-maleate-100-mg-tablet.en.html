Trimebutine maleate 100 mg

This medicine is used for acid reflux and hiatal hernia; for the digestive problems that come with gastritis, duodenitis or ulcers — stomach pain, indigestion, nausea and vomiting; and for irritable bowel syndrome and spastic colon.

Why this one

A digestive aid that regulates gut movement

How to take it

Adults take one to two tablets (100–200 mg) three times a day, before meals. The dose may be adjusted to suit your age and symptoms.

Before you take this

  • Do not take this if you have an inherited problem such as galactose intolerance, Lapp lactase deficiency or glucose-galactose malabsorption.
  • Talk to a pharmacist before taking it if you are pregnant, may be pregnant, or are breastfeeding.
  • Talk to a pharmacist or doctor if your symptoms last a long time or get worse.
