Made by Korea Biopharm HP · 2 times a day, 1 tablet per serving

This health functional food combines Calcium, Magnesium. Calcium is an MFDS-recognized ingredient needed for the formation of bones and teeth, for maintaining nerve and muscle function and normal blood clotting, and adequate intake may help reduce the risk of osteoporosis. Magnesium is an MFDS-recognized ingredient needed for energy utilization and for maintaining nerve and muscle function.
