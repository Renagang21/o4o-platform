Made by Novarex · Once a day, 1 tablet per serving

This health functional food combines Vitamin D, Vitamin K. Vitamin D is an MFDS-recognized ingredient needed for the absorption and use of calcium and phosphorus and for the formation and maintenance of bone, and may help reduce the risk of osteoporosis. Vitamin K is an MFDS-recognized ingredient needed for normal blood clotting and for bone composition.
