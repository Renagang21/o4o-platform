CalmadiCalcium + Magnesium + Vitamin D · Bone health

Made by E&S · Once a day, 2 tablets per serving

This health functional food combines Calcium, Magnesium, Vitamin D. Calcium is an MFDS-recognized ingredient needed for the formation of bones and teeth, for maintaining nerve and muscle function and normal blood clotting, and adequate intake may help reduce the risk of osteoporosis. Magnesium is an MFDS-recognized ingredient needed for energy utilization and for maintaining nerve and muscle function. Vitamin D is an MFDS-recognized ingredient needed for the absorption and use of calcium and phosphorus and for the formation and maintenance of bone, and may help reduce the risk of osteoporosis. Each ingredient is recognized by Korea's MFDS, and the labelled amount and recognized function of each are shown below.
