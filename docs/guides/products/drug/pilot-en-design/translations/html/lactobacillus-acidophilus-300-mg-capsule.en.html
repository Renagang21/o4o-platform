Lactobacillus acidophilus 300 mg

This medicine is used to settle the gut and to help with constipation, loose stools, bloating and abnormal fermentation in the bowel.

At a glance

Category

Over-the-counter

Ingredient

Lactobacillus acidophilus 300 mg

How it works

Settles the gut (improves the balance of gut bacteria)

Main symptoms

Constipation, loose stools, bloating, abnormal fermentation in the bowel

Who should be careful

Weakened immune system; babies

Why this one

A live-culture treatment that helps balance the gut

How to take it

Adults and children 12 or older take one capsule (300 mg) one to three times a day. Children aged 8 to under 12 take it once or twice a day.

Before you take this

  • Do not take this if your immune system is weakened (for example by AIDS, leukaemia, an organ transplant or high-dose steroids), or for a child under 1 who has a high fever or blood in the stool.
  • Talk to a pharmacist before taking it if you have ever reacted to Yellow No. 5.
