Why this one

An anti-diarrhoeal that stops diarrhoea

How to take it

Adults take two capsules to start, then one more capsule each time they pass a loose stool, without going over the stated daily dose. Replace fluids and salts while taking it. If sudden diarrhoea has not improved within 48 hours, or long-lasting diarrhoea within 10 days, stop taking it and get advice.

Before you take this

  • Do not take this for babies under 2, for bacterial diarrhoea with blood in the stool or a high fever, or if you have sudden ulcerative colitis, pseudomembranous colitis, a blocked bowel or megacolon.
  • Talk to a pharmacist before taking it if you have liver problems, are elderly, or are pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • If you feel tired, dizzy or sleepy after taking it, take care when driving or operating machinery.
