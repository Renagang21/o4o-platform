Acetaminophen 325 mg

This medicine is used for fever and pain from a cold, headache, nerve pain, muscle pain, period pain, sprain pain, toothache and joint pain.

At a glance

Category

Over-the-counter

Ingredient

Acetaminophen 325 mg

How it works

Reduces fever and pain

Main symptoms

Fever, headache, nerve pain, muscle pain, period pain, toothache, joint pain

Who should be careful

Liver disease; alcohol; do not exceed 4,000 mg a day

Why this one

A fever and pain reliever that is gentle on the stomach (soft capsule)

How to take it

Adults and children 12 or older take two capsules three to four times a day, every 4 to 6 hours as needed. Do not take more than 12 capsules (4 g) in one day. Take the lowest dose for the shortest possible time.

Before you take this

  • Do not take this if you have ever reacted to it, if you have a peptic ulcer, severe liver or kidney problems, aspirin-induced asthma, or if you drink alcohol.
  • If you drink alcohol regularly there is a risk of liver damage, so get advice.
  • Do not take it together with other products that contain acetaminophen.
  • Get advice if a fever lasts more than 3 days or pain more than 10 days.
