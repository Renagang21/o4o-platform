Almagate 500 mg

This medicine neutralises stomach acid and eases symptoms of stomach and duodenal ulcers, gastritis, excess stomach acid, heartburn, nausea, vomiting, stomach pain and acid burping.

At a glance

Category

Over-the-counter

Ingredient

Almagate 500 mg

How it works

Neutralises stomach acid

Main symptoms

Heartburn, excess stomach acid, acid burping, stomach pain, nausea

Who should be careful

Kidney problems; take care with long-term use

Why this one

An antacid that is chewed

How to take it

Adults and children 12 or older chew two tablets (1 g) three times a day, 30 minutes to 1 hour after meals. If needed, one more dose may be taken before bed.

Before you take this

  • Do not take this if you have ever reacted to it, if you have Alzheimer's disease, or if you have bleeding in the digestive tract that has not been diagnosed.
  • Talk to a pharmacist if you have kidney problems, are elderly, have trouble absorbing food, or have signs of bleeding such as black stools.
  • Do not take it together with tetracycline antibiotics.
  • If your symptoms do not improve after about 2 weeks, stop taking it and talk to a pharmacist or doctor.
