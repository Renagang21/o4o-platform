Symptoms of allergic rhinitis

Who should be careful

Kidney failure (lower dose); pregnancy and breastfeeding

Why this one

An antihistamine that is less likely to make you sleepy

How to take it

Adults and teenagers 12 or older take one tablet with water before a meal. If you have kidney failure, start with half a tablet once a day. Do not take it with grapefruit or orange juice, or with an antacid.

Before you take this

  • Do not take this if you have ever reacted to it.
  • Talk to a pharmacist before taking it if you have kidney failure, are a child under 12, are elderly, have had heart or blood vessel disease, or are pregnant or breastfeeding.
