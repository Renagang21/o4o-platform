Citrulline malate 500 mg

This medicine is used as a supportive treatment for functional weakness.

At a glance

Category

Over-the-counter

Ingredient

Citrulline malate 500 mg

How it works

Supports treatment for functional weakness

Main symptoms

Weakness and tiredness from reduced function

Who should be careful

Under 6; do not take for more than 4 weeks

Why this one

A preparation used to support treatment for functional weakness

How to take it

Adults take two tablets three times a day. Children 6 or older take two tablets twice a day. Do not take it for more than 4 weeks.

Before you take this

  • Do not give this to a child under 6.
  • Talk to a pharmacist before taking it if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, or have ever reacted to Yellow No. 203 or Yellow No. 5.
  • If there is no improvement after about a month, stop taking it.
