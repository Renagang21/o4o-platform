Sobrerol 200 mg

This medicine is used for sudden and long-lasting bronchitis, bronchitis with asthma or a cough, inflammation of the nose and throat, and inflammation of the voice box and windpipe — that is, sudden and long-lasting breathing problems with increased mucus.

At a glance

Category

Over-the-counter

Ingredient

Sobrerol 200 mg

How it works

Thins mucus and helps you cough it up

Main symptoms

Sudden and long-lasting bronchitis; breathing problems with increased mucus

Who should be careful

Under 2; pregnancy and breastfeeding

Why this one

A mucus-thinning treatment that helps clear phlegm

How to take it

Adults take one capsule (200 mg) twice a day.

Before you take this

  • Do not take this if you have ever reacted to it.
  • Talk to a pharmacist before taking it if you are pregnant, may be pregnant, are breastfeeding, or for a baby under 2.
  • Get advice if the phlegm or cough lasts a long time.
