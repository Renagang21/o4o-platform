Cetirizine hydrochloride 10 mg

This medicine is used for seasonal and year-round allergic rhinitis, allergic conjunctivitis, long-lasting hives with no known cause, and itchy skin.

At a glance

Category

Over-the-counter

Ingredient

Cetirizine hydrochloride 10 mg

How it works

Antihistamine (eases allergy symptoms)

Main symptoms

Allergic rhinitis and conjunctivitis, long-lasting hives, itchy skin

Who should be careful

Kidney failure; pregnancy and breastfeeding; sleepiness

Why this one

An antihistamine taken once a day (soft capsule)

How to take it

Adults and children 6 or older take 10 mg once a day, before bed. If you are sensitive to side effects, you may take 5 mg in the morning and 5 mg in the evening.

Before you take this

  • Do not take this if you have ever reacted to it, if you have kidney failure, or if you are under 6.
  • Talk to a pharmacist before taking it if you have kidney or liver problems, are elderly, are at risk of epilepsy or seizures, or are pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • It can make you sleepy, so take care when driving or operating machinery.
  • Do not take it together with theophylline or a large amount of alcohol.
