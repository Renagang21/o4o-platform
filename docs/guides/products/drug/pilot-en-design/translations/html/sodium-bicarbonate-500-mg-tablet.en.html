Sodium bicarbonate 500 mg

This medicine neutralises stomach acid and eases symptoms of stomach and duodenal ulcers, gastritis and excess stomach acid. It is also used to correct acidosis and to help the body pass uric acid.

An antacid that neutralises stomach acid quickly

How to take it

Adults take one to two tablets (0.5–1 g) at a time, 6–10 tablets (3–5 g) a day. The dose may be adjusted to suit your age and symptoms.

Before you take this

  • Do not take this if you need to limit how much sodium you take in — for example with high blood sodium, swelling, pregnancy-related high blood pressure, or high blood pressure.
  • Talk to a pharmacist before taking it if you have kidney problems, heart problems or congestive heart failure.
  • Taking it with a lot of milk or calcium supplements can cause milk-alkali syndrome, so get advice.
