This medicine is used to prevent pregnancy. It has a number of important restrictions and interacts with other medicines, so talk to a pharmacist before you start.

How to take it

Take one tablet every day at about the same time, with water, following the order marked on the pack. Take the first tablet on the first day of your period. Keep taking one every day even if you have bleeding, and start the next pack straight after the one before.Always check the full instructions with a pharmacist.

Before you take this

  • Do not take this if you have an active blood clot in a vein.
  • Do not take this if you have severe liver disease that has not returned to normal.
  • Do not take this if you have a cancer that is known or suspected to be sensitive to sex hormones.
  • Do not take this if you have vaginal bleeding that has not been diagnosed.
  • Do not take this if you are pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • Talk to a pharmacist before taking it if you have had breast cancer, or if you have liver problems, diabetes, high blood pressure or depression, or if you smoke.
  • It interacts with medicines that induce liver enzymes and with some antibiotics and antifungals — tell your pharmacist about every medicine you are taking.
  • Get advice if you notice mood changes, breast pain or irregular periods.
