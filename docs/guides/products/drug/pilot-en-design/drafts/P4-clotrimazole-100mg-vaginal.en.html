Clotrimazole 100 mg Vaginal TabletA thrush treatment inserted into the vagina

Inserted into the vagina — never swallowed · Adults

This medicine is used for vaginal thrush (candidal vaginitis). Although it is called a tablet, it is inserted into the vagina and is never swallowed.

What it helps with

  • Vaginal thrush (candidal vaginitis)

At a glance

Ingredient

Clotrimazole 100 mg

Vaginal tablet. Category: over-the-counter medicine.

How it works

Antifungal

Stops the growth of candida.

Main symptoms

Vaginal thrush

Candidal vaginitis.

Why this one

Treats where it acts

Inserted into the vagina, where the infection is.

Who should be careful

Never swallow

Not during your period. Ask a pharmacist if you are pregnant.

Category

Over-the-counter

Available without a prescription at the pharmacy counter.

How to use it

Adults insert one tablet high into the vagina once a day, at bedtime. The 100 mg strength is used for 6 days in a row.Use this tablet in the vagina only — do not swallow it, and keep it away from your eyes. It can weaken latex and rubber products such as condoms.

Before you use this

  • Do not use this if you have ever reacted to it.
  • Do not use it during your period.
  • Talk to a pharmacist before using it if you are pregnant, may be pregnant, or are breastfeeding.
  • While using it, do not also use tampons, douches or other products placed in the vagina.
  • Stop using it and get advice if you have a high fever, lower abdominal pain, or discharge with a bad smell.
