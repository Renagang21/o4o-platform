Officially recognised functions by ingredient

  • Magnesium
    • Needed for energy utilisation
    • Needed to help maintain normal nerve and muscle function
  • Biotin
    • Needed for the metabolism of fat, carbohydrate and protein and for energy production
  • Vitamin C
    • Needed for the formation and maintenance of connective tissue
    • Needed for the absorption of iron
    • Needed to protect cells from harmful oxygen (free radicals)
  • Vitamin D
    • Needed for the absorption and utilisation of calcium and phosphorus
    • Needed for the formation and maintenance of bone
    • Recognised as helping reduce the risk of developing osteoporosis
  • Selenium
    • Needed to protect cells from harmful oxygen (free radicals)
  • Zinc
    • Needed for normal immune function
    • Needed for normal cell division
  • Folate
    • Needed for cell and blood formation
    • Needed for the normal development of the fetal neural tube
    • Needed to help maintain normal blood homocysteine levels
  • Pantothenic acid
    • Needed for the metabolism of fat, carbohydrate and protein and for energy production
