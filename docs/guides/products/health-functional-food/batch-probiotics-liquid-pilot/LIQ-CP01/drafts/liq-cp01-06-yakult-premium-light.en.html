A liquid probiotic for those caring for their gut health. The labelled standard is at least 10 billion CFU per 100ml, and the official direction is once a day, one bottle. The volume of one bottle is not stated in the official text, so this page does not calculate a per-bottle count. Probiotics are recognized by Korea's MFDS as an ingredient that may help promote lactic acid bacteria growth and suppress harmful bacteria, may help support smooth bowel movement, and may help support gut health.
