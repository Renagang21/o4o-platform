Made by Huons EN · Enteric hard capsule · 2 capsules (900 mg) once a day

The labelled standard for this product is at least 100 million CFU per 900 mg, and the official directions are 2 capsules (900 mg) once a day — the labelled basis and the serving are both 900 mg. The weight of a single capsule is not stated in the official text, so this page does not calculate a per-capsule count. Probiotics are recognized by Korea's MFDS as an ingredient that may help promote lactic acid bacteria growth and suppress harmful bacteria, may help support smooth bowel movement, and may help support gut health.
