Made by E&S · Once a day, 1 tablet per serving

This health functional food combines Vitamin C, Vitamin D. Vitamin C is an MFDS-recognized ingredient needed for the formation and maintenance of connective tissue, for the absorption of iron, and for antioxidant protection of cells from free radicals. Vitamin D is an MFDS-recognized ingredient needed for the absorption and use of calcium and phosphorus and for the formation and maintenance of bone, and may help reduce the risk of osteoporosis. Each ingredient is recognized by Korea's MFDS, and the labelled amount and recognized function of each are shown below.
